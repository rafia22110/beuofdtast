The integration of AI into ophthalmology is primarily data-driven. To realize the potential of AI not only in diagnosing eye diseases but also in predicting systemic diseases, the medical community must adopt advanced data sharing methods that balance the need for diverse and large-scale data with the protection of patient privacy. The use of methods such as federated learning and synthetic data, along with ensuring demographic diversity to prevent bias, is key to developing more personalized, equitable, and accurate medicine.
